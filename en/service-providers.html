Service Providers

As of: [publication date]

This list names the external service providers we use for the website anora-systems.com and for SpecScout. Which data they receive and on which legal basis is described in our privacy policy; clause 11(3) of our GTC refers to this list. When a service provider changes, we update the list and the date above.

We base transfers to countries outside the EU on an adequacy decision of the European Commission, for the United Kingdom and, for the USA, under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF) where the recipient is certified under it, and otherwise on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (SCC). Details are in section A.6 of the privacy policy.
