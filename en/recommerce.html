How SpecScout fits your intake

  1. 1

    Intake and grading

    You check and grade the device as you do today. Condition, battery health and grading stay with you.

  2. 2

    Add the model's data

    Model name and brand, or an EAN, is enough: SpecScout returns the attributes you define, in your units and value lists.

  3. 3

    Create the listing

    Complete, filterable attributes for your shop and your marketplace listings. Through the API for whole intake lists too, one request per device.

Older models follow the same rules as new ones: a value is only returned when two independent sources agree, or when an authoritative source such as the manufacturer confirms it. Where a model is barely documented any more, the result shows it, as "not found" instead of a guess.
