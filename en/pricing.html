Good to know

What counts as a search?

A search is one product with the specs you ask for. Only completed searches count toward your allowance. Monthly allowances renew on the first day of each month; unused searches expire.

When do I need Enterprise?

Free is for testing and private use. If you use SpecScout in your business on an ongoing basis, for example to enrich your catalog regularly, you need Enterprise. Results you got with Free are yours to keep using.

Large catalogs

Process your catalog through the API, one request per product. Or send us your product list: we run it for you and return the results with a short quality report.

Contract and invoices

Enterprise is agreed in an individual contract; the offer states your allowance and price. Invoices are payable within 14 days. Our Terms and Conditions apply.
