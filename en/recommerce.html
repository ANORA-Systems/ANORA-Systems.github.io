Transparent

Nobody can check "fast processor and a big screen". SpecScout gives you the sources and a confidence score for every value. A value is only returned when two independent sources agree or an authoritative one such as the manufacturer confirms it, for older models too. Otherwise it says "not found" instead of guessing.

Filterable

That the device on the left supports eSIM is only in the description. Anyone filtering your shop by eSIM or screen size won't find it. SpecScout fills the attributes you define, in your units and value lists, so every device has the same fields, new or used.

As sellable as new

Buyers compare a used device with the new one, attribute by attribute. When your listing has the same data as the new product, condition and price decide. And on price, the used device comes out ahead.
